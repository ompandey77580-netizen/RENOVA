In detail

What happens at each step

Share your project brief

Tell us the room, its measurements and your budget band — then upload a few photos so pros see exactly what they're quoting for. You can pick ideas straight from the Inspiration gallery and attach them to your brief. Pro tip: add 3–5 reference photos from the Inspiration gallery for 40% more accurate quotes.

Meet your matches

Verified pros respond within 24 hours with their availability and a short intro video, so you know who you'd be working with before any site visit. Every profile shows past projects, ratings and specialisations. Pro tip: check the 'Verified' badge — those pros passed ID + trade-skill audits.

Compare quotes transparently

Every quote is benchmarked against Renova's live rate database — materials and labour, line by line — so you instantly spot overpricing. Compare timelines, warranties and payment terms side by side in one view. Pro tip: quotes within ±10% of our estimator are tagged 'Fair Price'.

Pay by milestone

Your money sits in secure escrow and releases only per completed stage — demolition, electrical, flooring and so on. Nothing moves until you sign off each milestone, keeping you in control from start to finish. Pro tip: always book the free mid-project inspection before the final release.

FAQ

Questions homeowners ask

Is Renova free for homeowners?
Yes. Browsing contractors, getting cost estimates and using our advisory tools are completely free for homeowners. We earn a small service fee from completed projects, so our incentives are aligned with yours.
How are contractors verified?
Every pro goes through ID and license checks, a trade-skill assessment, and a 3-project review audit with past clients before their profile goes live. Verification is re-checked periodically.
How accurate is the cost estimator?
Within ±10% for most projects. The estimator uses live material rates and standard labour costs for your city; premium and luxury finishes are shown as separate multipliers so you can see the impact before committing.
What if something goes wrong mid-project?
Because payments sit in milestone escrow, unfinished work simply doesn't get paid. We re-match a new verified pro for the remaining scope and cover re-quote costs up to ₹10,000.
Can Renova help with small jobs?
Absolutely — from a single light-fitting (₹500) to full-home renovations (₹10L+). The same matching, transparent quoting and milestone protection applies at every budget.
Which cities do you cover?
We currently operate in 12 metros including Pune, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad — with 30+ cities planned by next year. Enter your pincode to check availability in your area.
