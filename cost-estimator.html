How we calculate

Estimate = (base rate per sq.ft × area × quality multiplier) + Σ (specific work add-ons × area)

Base rates per sq.ft: Kitchen ₹1,200 · Bathroom ₹1,500 · Bedroom ₹800 · Living ₹900 · Full home ₹1,000.

Quality multipliers: Standard ×1.0 · Premium ×1.35 · Luxury ×1.8.

Work add-ons per sq.ft: Flooring ₹250 · Painting ₹120 · Plumbing ₹180 · Electrical ₹200 · Carpentry ₹220.

We show a ±9% range because site conditions vary.
